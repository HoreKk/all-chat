# Match hours in Paris time

Research for the match-hours ticket on the [wayfinder map](https://github.com/HoreKk/all-chat/issues/1). Checked on 2026-10-10 against [Insider Gaming's start-times article](https://insider-gaming.com/lol-worlds-2026-start-times-and-full-schedule-revealed/), which reports Riot's published schedule. [rdy.gg](https://rdy.gg/en/lol/news/league-of-legends-lol-worlds-2026-details), the brief's second source, returned 403. A search summary ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/79302-league-of-legends-worlds-2026-schedule-teams-format-venues-and-where-to-watch), [lolesports primer](https://lolesports.com/news/worlds-2026-primer)) agrees on venues and formats. The owner confirms the late knockout hours: they start around 23:00 Paris and finish around 03:00–04:00.

## Short answer

- Every first match starts in the Paris **evening**, between 18:00 and 23:00. **Late matches run past midnight Paris** on Play-Ins days, on Oct 28–30 and on every knockout day. This makes the brief's "late games end after midnight Paris" risk certain, not Low.
- Europe leaves summer time on Oct 25, but the US only does on Nov 1. So between Oct 25 and Oct 31 the gap is one hour shorter. The article mislabels two Swiss blocks as CEST. The Paris times below are recomputed from the PDT/CDT times.

There are no matches on Oct 19–22, Oct 27, Nov 1–2 or Nov 9–13.

## Past midnight Paris

- **Certain:** every quarterfinal and semifinal day. A best-of-five starting at 23:00 ends around 03:00–04:00, as the owner confirms.
- **Likely:** the Play-Ins days (best-of-five from 20:00) and Oct 28–30 (from 21:00).
- **Probably not:** the 18:00 and 19:00 Swiss days. The Final starts at 20:00 as a best-of-five, so it may end around midnight.

## What the sources do not give

- Matches per day, last-match times and per-day best-of formats. The past-midnight estimates come from the formats in the search summary: Play-Ins best-of-five, Swiss best-of-one then best-of-three, knockouts best-of-five.
- Swiss pairings, which are only known round by round.
- Any time for the Oct 19–22 gap, because nothing is played.
